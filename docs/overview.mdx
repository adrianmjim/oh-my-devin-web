Oh My Devin (`omd`) is an organizational layer on top of the Devin CLI. It turns
local Devin sessions into an organization of agents: functional **roles**,
declarative **teams**, and deliberative **councils**. The Devin CLI supplies the
execution engine; `omd` adds the orchestrator, the organizational structure, and
the protocols — role contracts, context isolation, and verifiable gates.

A single long session has no first-class model for reusable functional roles,
stable team compositions, structured handoffs, context separation between
stages, independent review, or gates that stop progress until there is
evidence. Oh My Devin adds exactly that model, without replacing the engine
underneath.

## How it works

Every unit of work runs as its own headless Devin session, launched and
supervised by `omd` from your terminal. A role is an enforced configuration
bundle — tools, permissions, model, and a validated output artifact — compiled
per invocation and passed to the engine, so the contract holds even when the
model ignores its instructions. Work crosses a role boundary only through
explicitly handed artifacts, never through shared conversation history.

The CLI is the orchestrator: it installs the in-session layer, launches and
supervises role, team, and council runs, and reports on runs in flight.

## What it is not

Oh My Devin is not a Devin replacement and not another session engine. It runs
the `devin` CLI you already use, locally, and stays out of the way of your
normal interactive sessions. The organizational layer is opt-in per project.

## Status

Oh My Devin is an early implementation. It is not yet published to a package
registry — you build it from source — and its surface may change between
releases.
